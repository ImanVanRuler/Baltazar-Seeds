THE
COMEBACK
WAS
MESSY.
01

Quality over quantity

The return was not designed as a perfect production project. The starting point was curiosity. What happens when a mixed collection of seeds is allowed to show what is actually in it.

02

It did not start smoothly

The first attempt began with 23 seeds and only three early plants. Dry winter air and heat became part of the real conditions. More seeds were prepared and the experiment continued.

03

Selection became unavoidable

As more plants appeared the differences became part of the record. One unusual plant even became number 18 in the Zwergenbattle 2025.

04

Every plant was different

Possible identities were treated as possibilities rather than absolute labels. Gelato. Skunk. Purple Tangie. Cali Orange. Variation remained the interesting part.
