The Beginning

It didn't start with a catalogue.
It started with a grow.

After more than ten years away from growing, the comeback began as a simple experiment. More than fifty seeds from mixed and uncertain origins were tested. Some germinated. Some didn't. Some plants disappeared from the selection. A few stood out.

That first run became the starting point for a bigger idea: observe what the plants actually give you, keep what is interesting and build from there.
