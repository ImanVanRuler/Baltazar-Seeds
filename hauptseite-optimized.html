Strainmix Part One

After more than a decade away from growing, the project begins again. Not as a polished breeding program and not as a high-end production run — but as a comeback. The stated goal from the start was simple: quality over quantity and the curiosity to see what an unpredictable seed mix would become.

THE MIX · UNKNOWN BY DESIGN

A box full of

Seed origin was part of the experiment

The mix was built from seeds collected over three years or longer. Most were older seeds from flowers — mainly Gelato — alongside seeds from a coffee-shop seed vending machine. The report also names Cali Orange, Gelato and Purple Tangie. Some seeds were free or cost about two euros.

JANUARY · THE STRUGGLE

It didn't start
smoothly.

23 planted · only 3 initially emerged

The first attempt used 23 seeds and only three small plants had emerged at first. Around thirty more seeds were then prepared. The report describes dry winter air, heating, lamp heat and suboptimal climate as part of the challenge. More than fifty seeds had been put through the process overall.

The mix became a catalogue of variation

By day 37 the plants were being tentatively identified by smell and appearance: possible Gelato, Skunk, Purple Tangie and Cali Orange among them. Later the report notes that every plant was different. The aromas ranged from sweet and herbal to intensely pungent, citrus and lemon.

15.04.2025 · BT. 57 · FINALE

Not the end.

A comeback run becomes a direction

The harvest came at BT. 57. Some plants could have stood longer but the run was intentionally brought to a close. The conclusion was clear: this was a learning run and the unstable genetics made the outcome unpredictable. The next seed mix was planned with ordered seeds from official breeders — followed later by a focused single-strain run and eventually the long-term goal of developing something of its own.
